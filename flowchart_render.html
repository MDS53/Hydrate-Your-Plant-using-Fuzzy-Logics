Fuzzy Logic Watering System

Application Decision & Calculation Flow

1. Data Collection

Gathers live weather conditions and plant baseline water requirements

2. Input Fuzzification

Converts raw weather readings into descriptive terms: Warm, Dry, Humid

3. Water Quantity Fuzzification

Defines water volume levels: Skip, Very Low, Low, Normal, High, Very High

4. Rule Evaluation

Applies IF-THEN rules linking weather conditions to water quantity levels

5. Defuzzification

Merges active water levels into a single precise watering factor

6. Context Adjustment

Fine-tunes the water factor by plant species and environment

7. Final Output

Calculates exact milliliters to pour and dispatches reminders
